Route

Our float from Moab to Lake Powell — every camp, every rapid

The Float Route

We launched at Moab, Utah and floated the Colorado River through Cataract Canyon, taking out at the Hite Marina area on Lake Powell. The route covers roughly 100 river miles and descends through 28 named rapids, including the legendary Big Drops.

Key Waypoints

Launch — Moab, UT — We put in at the Moab boat launch on the Colorado River, roughly 1.5 miles downstream of the US-191 bridge.

Confluence — At river mile 68 below Green River, UT (mile 0 below the confluence), the Green River joins the Colorado. The water changes color here — the Colorado runs redder, the Green runs cleaner. Below the confluence the river enters Cataract Canyon proper.

Big Drops — Rapids 21–23, roughly 14 miles below the confluence, are the climax of the canyon. Scout from river left before committing to any line.

Flat Water / Lake Powell — Below the Big Drops the gradient eases. Depending on reservoir levels, the flatwater of Lake Powell begins anywhere from 5 to 25 miles below the Big Drops.

Takeout — Hite Area — A motorized shuttle boat meets us on the lake for the final crossing to the takeout road.
