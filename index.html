Cataract Canyon 2026

Six families. One river. A canyon carved in stone and memory.

Welcome to the trip site

In May 2026, six families launched their boats into the Colorado River and ran Cataract Canyon — one of the most storied stretches of whitewater in the American West. This site collects the maps, stories, and photos from that adventure, shared with the people who were there.

Use the navigation above to explore the natural and human history of the river, follow our route on the map, read the day-by-day trip log, browse each family's photo album, and meet the crew.
